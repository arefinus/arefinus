# Md Sultanul Arefin Sourav

Business analytics researcher. Data science, data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and business automation.

Business analytics research with an academic affiliation to Trine University. IIBA Member. My work looks at how data science, machine learning, and automation can support business decisions that people can inspect: fraud review, operational planning, receivables, demand and inventory, customer analytics, and model monitoring.

Portfolio and interactive demonstrations: https://arefinus.github.io

## Projects

Every repository runs offline on CPU from authored synthetic fixtures, carries tests and CI, documents its data access, and labels every number as `paper-reported`, `reproduced`, `demo`, or `proposed`. None bundles a dataset. Implementation was AI-assisted and reviewed before release; see each repository's NOTICE.md.

**Research implementations**

- [templar-fraud-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/templar-fraud-lab): routed, verifier-grounded, calibrated and cost-sensitive fraud decision pipeline, reimplemented independently with a fidelity audit against the published protocol.
- [attention-qelm-gwo-iiot-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/attention-qelm-gwo-iiot-lab): attention encoder with a fixed-weight extreme learning machine head and grey wolf hyperparameter search for IIoT intrusion detection, with a fidelity audit and an ambiguity log.

**Business operations and finance**

- [business-flow-simulator](https://github.com/arefinus/business-flow-simulator): deterministic discrete-event simulator of a fictional supplier-to-payment workflow with idempotent events, review queues and policy comparison.
- [cashflow-invoice-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/cashflow-invoice-lab): three-way matching, duplicate invoice checks, payment-delay baselines and a 13-week cash forecast with intervals.
- [sec-business-evidence](https://github.com/arefinus/sec-business-evidence): cached SEC EDGAR company-facts ingestion with source-cited ratios and review flags, never a fraud verdict.
- [demand-inventory-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/demand-inventory-lab): forecasting baselines with rolling-origin backtests and a synthetic replenishment simulator.

**Customers, text and monitoring**

- [customer-retention-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/customer-retention-lab): leakage-controlled churn-risk demonstration with point-in-time features, cohorts, calibration and capacity-limited ranking.
- [complaint-intelligence-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/complaint-intelligence-lab): complaint categorisation and review routing on the CFPB schema with abstention and chronology-aware evaluation.
- [model-monitoring-lab](https://github.com/arefinus/model-monitoring-lab): drift, calibration and data-quality monitoring with a documented alert state machine and delayed-label handling.
